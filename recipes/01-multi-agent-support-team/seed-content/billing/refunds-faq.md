# Refunds — Frequently Asked Questions

## Annual plans

### How long do I have to request a refund?

Annual plans qualify for a prorated refund within **14 days** of the renewal
date. After day 14, the plan is non-refundable for that year.

### What "prorated" means here

The refund is `(days_remaining / 365) × annual_price`, rounded to the nearest
cent. If you've used more than 14 days of the new cycle, you're past the
window regardless of usage.

### How long until the refund shows on my card?

Refunds process within 1 business day on our side. Your bank typically
reflects the credit in 3–10 business days, depending on the card network.

## Monthly plans

### Can I get a refund on a monthly charge?

Monthly plans are **not refundable**. If you cancel mid-cycle, your service
continues until the end of the paid period and does not renew.

### Exception for billing errors

If you were double-charged, billed after cancelling, or charged due to a
system error on our side, we refund in full. Contact billing@acme.example
with the invoice number; we respond within 1 business day.

## Enterprise contracts

Enterprise contracts follow the terms negotiated in your Order Form. Refunds
outside those terms are handled case-by-case by your Account Executive.

## Partial refunds for downtime

If our service has documented downtime exceeding 0.1% of the month (~43
minutes) and you're on the Business or Enterprise plan, you qualify for an
SLA credit on your next invoice. The credit is calculated automatically and
does not require a refund request.

## How to request

Email billing@acme.example with:

1. Account email or organization ID
2. Invoice number (visible in **Settings → Billing → Invoices**)
3. Reason for the request

We respond within 1 business day.
